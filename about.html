OUR STORY

Bringing the right minds together since 2012.

The Ghana Economic Forum brings leaders from business, public policy, and academia into a shared discussion about national development.

Created in 2012, the forum provides space to examine economic challenges, explore their causes, and consider practical responses. It is an initiative of the Business & Financial Times.

Entrepreneurship, innovation, and collaboration are central to the conversation. The aim is to turn informed debate into opportunities for sustainable progress.

01 / OUR PURPOSE

Put economic progress on the agenda.

Connect experience across sectors to examine Ghana’s economic priorities and identify pathways towards lasting prosperity.

02 / OUR APPROACH

Build understanding through dialogue.

Bring different perspectives into the same room, ask useful questions, and create the connections that support action.
