About this website

This website presents information about the Ghana Economic Forum. It includes a contact form for enquiries. There is no account creation or newsletter subscription form.

Contacting the team

Email and telephone links let you contact the forum through your own email or phone service. The contact form asks for your name, email address, subject and message, with optional phone and organization details. These details are sent to the forum team through Resend to respond to your enquiry. Please avoid including sensitive personal information. For questions about your information, contact info@ghanaeconomicforum.com.

External services

Registration is handled on the external B&FT payment website. Social media and map links open external services. Review their privacy information before providing personal information.

Website assets

Images, styles, and application scripts are served with this website. When online messaging is configured, Cloudflare Turnstile provides a security check and the contact API uses your IP address for abuse prevention. Resend processes contact messages for email delivery. The website does not store contact messages in a separate application database. Email messages remain with the receiving team and service providers; contact the team about access or deletion. This version does not include analytics or advertising scripts. Hosting and service providers may process technical request data.
