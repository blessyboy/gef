MORE THAN A CONVERSATION

Where perspectives meet.
And progress begins.

GEF 2026 connects policy leaders, investors and industry to turn economic stabilization into industrial growth. The forum focuses on capital mobilization, manufacturing, technology adoption, agriculture and agro-processing, infrastructure development, and energy competitiveness.

Convened by the Business & Financial Times, it advances private sector development, investment readiness, strategic partnerships and export growth through the 24-Hour Economy and AfCFTA opportunities.

Discover the GEF 2026 vision ↗

KEY FORUM PRIORITIES

Financing Transformation. Scaling Industry.

Eight priorities for Ghana's next industrial wave. GEF 2026 brings together policymakers, investors and industry leaders to accelerate economic growth.

01 / GEF 2026

Mobilizing Capital

Connect domestic and international investment with productive sectors, long-term financing and business growth.

02 / GEF 2026

Industrial Growth

Advance economic transformation through industrialization, competitive production and private sector development.

03 / GEF 2026

Manufacturing & Value Addition

Expand local manufacturing and value chains to create jobs, strengthen domestic production and grow exports.

04 / GEF 2026

Technology & Innovation

Accelerate technology adoption, automation, artificial intelligence and innovation in productive enterprises.

05 / GEF 2026

Agriculture & Agro-processing

Link agriculture with manufacturing to support agro-processing, food production and value-added exports.

06 / GEF 2026

Energy Competitiveness

Build reliable, affordable and secure energy that supports industrial productivity and investment.

07 / GEF 2026

Infrastructure & Logistics

Mobilize investment and public-private partnerships for industrial infrastructure, transport and logistics.

08 / GEF 2026

Export Development

Strengthen export competitiveness and help Ghanaian enterprises access opportunities under AfCFTA.
