Investment Marketplace & Deal Room

FROM DIALOGUE TO INVESTMENT ENGAGEMENT

Connect projects with capital and partners.

The GEF Investment Marketplace & Integrated Deal Room connects business leaders and project owners with institutions, investors, technology companies and strategic partners.

The platform facilitates conversations about investment opportunities, project financing, strategic partnerships and expressions of interest. It supports investment readiness and private sector development across manufacturing, agriculture, infrastructure, energy, technology and export-oriented industries.

DAY 1 / INVESTMENT ROUNDTABLE

A focused dialogue on the 24-Hour Economy.

The GEF Deal Room: Investment Roundtable is part of the wider Marketplace. It brings a select group of leading Ghanaian business leaders into direct discussion with government about private capital mobilization, policy reforms, productive capacity and public-private partnerships.

Participation in this Roundtable is strictly by invitation and limited to 50 leading Ghanaian business leaders. The objective is to identify specific opportunities, financing requirements and partnerships.
